01 — Overview

One system, built to survive every surface it lands on.

Action Target sells complex, high-consideration equipment to a customer base that meets the brand in wildly different places — a convention hall, a printed catalog, a range bag, a screen. The campaign needed to hold together across all of them without flattening into one repeated logo.

The answer was a modular kit: a fixed set of marks, a tight blue-and-graphite palette, and a topographic pattern language that could stretch across a twelve-foot display or shrink to a two-inch patch and still read as the same brand.

02 — Apparel

An all-over print that reads at ten feet.

The apparel program pushed the pattern language furthest. Stars, stripes, and the topographic motif were rebuilt as a full-bleed sublimation print, tuned so the flag reads clearly at a distance and dissolves into texture up close.

The same artwork was re-cut for the neck gaiter, keeping the merch line visually consistent across two very different garment shapes.
