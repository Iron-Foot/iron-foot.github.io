Mafia Cards

A role card set for the party game Mafia: painted characters for the Detective, the Mafia and the Citizen, with an art-deco back that gives nothing away.

  • TypeCard game
  • DeliverablesThree painted role cards, deco card back, looping motion piece
  • RolesDetective, Mafia, Citizen

The set

Three roles, one back

Poker size · gold on black

01 — Overview

The back has to keep a secret. The face has to sell the part.

Mafia is played on bluffing, so the card back is the most important surface in the set. A symmetrical art-deco pattern in gold on black gives nothing away and looks the same whichever way it is dealt.

Each role gets a full-figure painted character in 1920s dress: a detective with a hand at his holster, a mafiosa with a pistol held behind her back, and a citizen who has no idea what is coming. The role name runs vertically down the edge so it can be read with the card barely tilted.

A short motion piece turns the cards over in sequence, the way they would be revealed at the table.
